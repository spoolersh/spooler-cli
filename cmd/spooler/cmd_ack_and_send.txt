Acknowledges a leased message and sends a new one atomically. The lease names
the message to acknowledge and --queue the queue to send to, the same one or
another. The payload is --data or --data-file; `--data-file -` reads it from
stdin. Prints the new message's id.

--delay postpones when the new message becomes visible. A send with the same
--dedup-string or --dedup-hash within the queue's dedup window is refused as a
whole, and nothing is acknowledged. --return-handle adds a handle to the new
message for inspect or discard.
