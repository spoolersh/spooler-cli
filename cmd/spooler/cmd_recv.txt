Receives and leases one message. Writes its payload to stdout or
--data-file. Specify at least one of --ack, --lease-file or --include-meta.

--ack acknowledges the message after the payload is fully written.
--lease-file saves the lease as JSON for ack, nack, release, fail or renew.
--include-meta prints the message's attributes on stdout, ahead of any
payload. If no message arrives within --wait, exits with code 1 and empty
stdout.
