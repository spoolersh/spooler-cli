Sends a message to a queue with the payload of --data or --data-file.
`--data-file -` reads it from stdin. Prints the message's id.

--delay postpones when the message becomes visible. A repeat send with
the same --dedup-string or --dedup-hash within the queue's dedup window is
reported as a duplicate instead of enqueued again. On a new send,
--return-handle adds a handle for inspect or discard.
